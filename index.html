Frequently asked questions

Is GPS Speedometer HUD free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use. A Speedometer HUD Pro subscription unlocks all features.

Where does the speed camera data come from?

Speed camera locations come from OpenStreetMap, made available under the Open Database License.

Do speed camera alerts work in the background?

No. Alerts show only while the app is open and in the foreground. Turn on Prevent Screen Locking in Settings to keep the app on screen while you drive.

Can I switch between km/h and mph?

Yes. Change the units in the app settings.
