Frequently asked questions

Is GPS Speedometer HUD free?

Yes. It is free to download and use, with an optional Speedometer HUD Pro subscription.

Where does the speed camera data come from?

From OpenStreetMap, a community map project, under the ODbL licence. Coverage depends on the data in your area.

Do the camera alerts work in the background?

Alerts work while the app is open and on screen. Keep the app open while you drive.

How accurate is the speed?

Speed comes from your iPhone GPS. It is a close estimate, not a certified reading. Always follow your car speedometer and the road signs.

Does it need an internet connection?

GPS speed works without data. The map and the camera data need a connection to load.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad and iPod touch on iOS 13 or later.
