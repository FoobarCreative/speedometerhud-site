About GPS Speedometer HUD

GPS Speedometer HUDFor iPhone and iPad. iOS 13 or later.

What the app does

GPS Speedometer HUD shows your current speed in large, clear digits, so you can read it at a glance from a phone mount. The speed comes from the GPS in your device.

  • Heads-up display. A big digital speed readout with your compass heading, in a dark layout that stays readable day and night.
  • Live map. A map of your area with known speed cameras marked on it.
  • Speed camera alerts. Turn on alerts and the app warns you as you approach a known speed camera.
  • Your units. Show your speed in km/h, mph or knots.

The speed shown is an estimate from GPS. It is not a certified reading. Always follow your car speedometer and the road signs.

Who makes it

GPS Speedometer HUD is made by Foobar Creative, an independent app studio in Adelaide, Australia. The app has been on the App Store since 2012.

Speed camera data

Speed camera locations come from OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License. Coverage depends on the data mapped in your area.
